The single most valuable card in sv3a Raging Surf is the Garchomp ex Special Illustration Rare (SIR), which was clearing $90-$120 in raw NM condition on eBay completed sales as of mid-2026.

Raging Surf (set code SV3A) released in Japan on September 22, 2023. It is a 92-card set built around the Tera Pokémon mechanic, headlined by Garchomp ex, Palafin ex, and a handful of Illustration Rares that have quietly held value better than most Scarlet and Violet-era Japanese sets. This guide ranks every card worth tracking, cross-references three pricing sources, and flags where graded copies change the calculus.

## Garchomp ex SIR (086/066): the set's anchor card

The Garchomp ex SIR is the card PSA auction data confirms has generated the most dollar volume from sv3a. PSA's publicly available auction totals for the full SV3A set logged $69,122 in total auction value across 1,832 sales, and Garchomp ex SIR accounts for a disproportionate share of the high-ticket transactions given its consistent $280-$340 PSA 10 range in mid-2026.

PokeBeard discussed the Garchomp ex SIR as one of his top-watched Scarlet and Violet-era Japanese cards, noting the card had come off a period of price softness and was showing renewed bidding activity. That is sentiment, not a price call, but it aligns with what completed sales show: the floor has held.

The PSA 10 premium over raw NM sits at roughly 2.8x to 3.2x. That is a wide enough spread to justify grading only if your raw copy grades cleanly. Centering issues are common on Japanese SV-era cards from this print run; a card with visible left-right imbalance will PSA 9 rather than 10 and the spread collapses to about 1.4x.

<Callout variant="tip">
If you are deciding whether to grade your Garchomp ex SIR, check centering first. A PSA 9 raw-to-graded flip at current prices nets you roughly $25-$40 after a standard submission fee, not worth the turnaround time unless you have a high-volume submission already going in.
</Callout>

## Palafin ex SIR (085/066): the second seat

Palafin ex SIR has settled into a $35-$50 raw range as of August 2026, down from a brief spike near $70 in late 2024 when the set was fresher and supply tighter. The PSA 10 ceiling of $110-$145 means the grading math is more favorable here than on Garchomp ex SIR, because PSA 10 copies are less abundant relative to demand.

The card's relative obscurity outside Japan works in buyers' favor. Palafin is not a legacy Pokémon, so collector demand is driven almost entirely by artwork quality rather than nostalgia. That typically means slower appreciation but also less speculator-driven volatility.

## Illustration Rares: where the overlooked value sits

SV3A's Illustration Rares have been quietly consistent. The Palafin IR (074/066) has held $12-$20 raw through most of 2026, which is solid for a card that does not feature a marquee Pokémon. At $40-$60 for a PSA 10, the grading premium is about 3x, the same multiple as Garchomp ex SIR but off a much lower base, which means submission costs eat more of the margin.

The Arven SIR (088/066) at $8-$14 raw is the set's most accessible entry point. Arven as a character has sustained collector interest across multiple sets, and the SV3A version benefits from that broad demand floor. If you are building a complete SIR set run, Arven is the one you add without overthinking it.

## Sealed product: booster box positioning

The sv3a Raging Surf booster box was listed on TCGplayer as of mid-2026 ranging from $70 (low-end seller) to $115 (premium listings with shipping included). That range reflects real condition and seller variance, not an error. Japanese booster boxes from SV3A are still in circulation from importers, so supply has not dried up the way it does for older sets.

For reference, a complete set of all SIR and SAR singles from sv3a would cost roughly $175-$220 purchased individually at current raw NM prices. A booster box at $80 gives you expected value well below that if you are pulling for hits, because box math on a 66-card base set with 26 extended slots does not favor set completion from sealed product.
