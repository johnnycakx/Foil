The most expensive card in sv3a Raging Surf is the Garchomp ex Special Illustration Rare (SIR), which has traded in the $90-$130 range for raw copies and climbs past $300 in PSA 10 as of 2026.

Raging Surf shipped in Japan on September 22, 2023, as a 92-card enhanced booster expansion anchored around Garchomp, Mew, Lugia, and Ting-Lu. Three years later it still pulls consistent collector demand because its SIR lineup is tight, the set never got an English equivalent, and sealed boxes have compressed to the $70-$115 range (per TCGplayer completed listings as of 2026), making singles the better purchase for most buyers. PriceCharting and Pokellector list prices but don't tell you which three fields to read off the card to confirm you have the right variant. Every card in this set shares a name with English counterparts; misidentification is common. The correct method: read the name, the set code (sv3a), and the collector number off the physical card. Artwork alone will get you into trouble, especially with the Garchomp ex cards where the standard ex, the RR, and the SIR share nearly identical templates at a glance.

## The sv3a SIR Tier: What the Top Slots Are Worth

Sv3a has four Special Illustration Rares. Here is where each one sits in 2026 based on TCGplayer market price and recent eBay sold comps:

| Card | Collector # | Raw (ungraded) | PSA 10 (approx.) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Garchomp ex SIR | 083/066 | $90-$130 | $280-$340 |
| Mew ex SIR | 084/066 | $55-$80 | $160-$220 |
| Lugia ex SIR | 085/066 | $45-$70 | $130-$190 |
| Ting-Lu ex SIR | 086/066 | $28-$45 | $80-$120 |

Garchomp ex SIR is the clear anchor. PokeBeard called it one of the more durable Scarlet and Violet era SIRs based on Garchomp's long competitive history, and that read holds up. The card's collector number is 083/066, above set total, which is the standard sv3a over-number format for high rarity cards. If the number on your card reads anything other than 083/066 and the name says Garchomp ex, you have a different and cheaper variant.

Mew ex SIR at 084/066 benefits from the 151 halo effect. Mew demand across Japanese sets has stayed elevated through 2025 and into 2026, partly because the sv2a 151 set drove new Mew collectors into the market who then backfill older Japanese Mew cards.

## Sealed Product: Booster Boxes and Single Packs

Sealed sv3a booster boxes opened 2026 with TCGplayer listings ranging from $70 to $115 per box depending on seller and shipping included. The K-TCG storefront had boxes listed at $138.99 as recently as mid-2026, which is on the high end. For reference, the last confirmed low on TCGplayer completed sales was $69.99 per box.

The math on cracking boxes is not favorable at current singles prices. A box yields 10 packs at 5 cards each. Hit rates for SIR rarity in Japanese enhanced boosters run roughly 1 SIR per box on average, meaning you're paying $90-$115 for a single SIR pull plus bulk. The Garchomp ex SIR raw at $90-$130 makes box cracking close to breakeven on a good pull, a loss on any other SIR. Buy singles.

## Graded Population and Grade Premium

PSA's auction record for sv3a cards shows total auction value of $69,122 across 1,832 sales (per PSA's published data). That's an average sale of roughly $38 per submitted card, which tells you most of the volume is mid-rarity bulk grading, not chase SIR submissions. ## Where sv3a Sits in the Broader Japanese Market

Raging Surf released in September 2023 as part of the early Scarlet and Violet Japanese wave. By 2025, the Japanese SV series had expanded significantly, and collector attention had partially rotated toward higher-profile sets like sv2a 151 and later releases. That rotation compressed sv3a singles prices from their 2023-2024 highs.

The stabilization is visible in the Garchomp ex SIR price history: it peaked above $150 raw shortly after release, corrected through 2024, and has held a tighter $90-$130 band through 2026. That's not recovery, that's a card finding its floor. For buyers, a floor is more useful than a spike; you know roughly what you're paying and the downside is limited.

The creator commentary space in mid-2026 is focused on upcoming sets like Destined Rivals and the 30th Anniversary wave rather than sv3a specifically. PokeBeard flagged Garchomp ex SIR as one of the more durable Scarlet and Violet era cards in recent coverage, but broader creator attention has rotated forward. When creator hype moves away from a set, prices tend to stabilize rather than spike; that's the environment sv3a is in right now.
