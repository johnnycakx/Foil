## What Makes sv3a Raging Surf Different from Other SV-Era Sets

Raging Surf is a 92-card Japanese set built almost entirely around the Paldea coastal region, with Garchomp ex as the mechanical centerpiece. What separates it from contemporaries like SV3 Obsidian Flames is the **concentration of value in a small number of SIR and SAR slots**. The set has exactly two Special Illustration Rares and one Art Rare worth tracking closely. That thin value distribution means a single pull in a booster box either makes the box or breaks it. There is no broad shallow floor of $15 to $20 rares cushioning a bad pull session.

Booster boxes were listed on TCGplayer as low as $69.99 sealed as of mid-2026, down from the $114.99 to $138.99 range some retailers still post. At $69.99, box math is punishing unless you hit a SIR.

### Tier 1: $80+, the cards that move the box EV

**Garchomp ex SIR (081/071)**
The headliner. Raw copies landed at $85 to $110 in recent completed sales, with heavy variance depending on centering. PSA 10 has cleared $220 to $260. PSA 9 sits considerably lower, around $130 to $150, which makes the grade cliff here worth noting: the PSA 10 premium is roughly 2x the PSA 9, not the usual 1.3x to 1.5x you see on less-demanded SIRs.

PokeBeard flagged Garchomp ex SIR as one of his tracked positions in July 2026, though his coverage was framed around whether the card had already priced in its demand floor. That is useful sentiment context, not a price signal.

**Gengar VMAX Alt Art: a common misattribution**
Gengar VMAX Alt is not a Raging Surf card. It belongs to S11 Lost Origin. Raging Surf is sometimes conflated with Lost Origin content in search results, so this is worth stating plainly. The Raging Surf Gengar representation is limited to standard treatment cards.

**Gardevoir ex SAR (080/071)**
The second heavy hitter. Raw copies have sold in the $60 to $80 range through mid-2026. PSA 10 population on this card remains thin (approximate as of 2026), which historically supports premium pricing when pop stays low. Gardevoir ex SAR is the card most frequently misidentified in photos because the full-art treatment bleeds into the border in a way that makes the collector number hard to read. Always confirm via the three-field method: card name, set code sv3a, and collector number 080.

<Callout variant="warning">
Identifying SIR and SAR cards by artwork alone is how misattributions happen. Raging Surf shares visual design language with SV3 Obsidian Flames on several full-art treatments. Read the set code (sv3a) and collector number off the card directly. Don't rely on artwork recognition.
</Callout>

### Tier 2: $20 to $60, the playable-value range

**Iono SAR (077/071)**
Iono's supporter card has held value well because the character appears across multiple SV-era sets and maintains crossover collector demand. Raw copies in this range sold for $30 to $50 through July 2026. PSA 10 comps have hit $90 to $100 at auction, making this one of the better grade-up candidates in the set if you can source raw copies with clean centering.

**Garchomp ex AR (052/071)**
The standard Art Rare treatment of Garchomp ex, sitting at $20 to $35 raw. Less upside than the SIR but far more liquid. This is the card that moves consistently rather than occasionally at high prices.

**A note on marketplace noise:** Arcanine ex SIR or SAR variants from other sets are sometimes cross-listed with sv3a in marketplace searches, inflating apparent set depth. Filter by collector number range 001 to 092 to get clean sv3a results.

### Tier 3: Under $20, the bulk-but-not-bulk range

Several Illustration Rares in the set, including Swampert ex IR and Camerupt ex IR, sit at $8 to $18 raw. These are worth sorting rather than bulk-binning, but grade economics rarely work at this price point unless you have a submission already going.
