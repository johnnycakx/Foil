<p>A PSA 9 Garchomp ex SIR from sv3a is quietly trading below raw NM. That's the most useful thing to know before you send anything to PSA from this set.</p>

<p>The new post maps every above-set card in sv3a Raging Surf by current price, graded ladder, and whether the grade premium actually holds. Here's the short version.</p>

<h2>Where the set sits in mid-2026</h2>

<p>Raging Surf has had nearly three years to settle since its September 2023 release. No active hype cycle, no creator consensus on a speculator play. That's not a knock on the set: it means you're buying against collectors, not flippers. The two SIRs carry the ceiling. <strong>Garchomp ex SIR (085/062) is moving raw NM at $45-$60</strong>, with PSA 10 copies reaching $130-$180. Mew ex SIR (086/062) runs a modest discount at $38-$52 raw, $110-$155 graded 10.</p>

<p>Below that, Palafin ex SAR sits at $18-$26 raw. Camerupt ex SAR is $8-$13. Neither is a speculator card right now. They're set-completion purchases and background dealer inventory.</p>

<h2>The grading math on Garchomp ex SIR</h2>

<p>This is where the post earns its keep. The grade ladder for Garchomp ex SIR:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>PSA 10:</strong> $130-$180</li>
  <li><strong>PSA 9:</strong> $55-$75</li>
  <li><strong>PSA 8:</strong> $28-$40</li>
  <li><strong>Raw NM:</strong> $45-$60</li>
</ul>

<p>The PSA 9 lands below raw NM in effective value. Buyers discount a graded 9 because they expected a 10. Raw buyers stay optimistic about their own eye test. It's a known inefficiency in Japanese SIR grading, and it hits sv3a hard because Japanese mini sets carry real print line and centering risk. A card with any surface issue doesn't make it to PSA 10, and a PSA 8 means you've paid grading fees to create something easier to sell than a raw copy, at a lower price.</p>

<p>The math works if you have high-confidence NM+ copies acquired below $40 and can hold through the turnaround cycle. It doesn't work if you're sending speculative raw pulls hoping for a 10.</p>

<h2>One number worth anchoring</h2>

<p>PSA's published auction data for this set shows 1,832 total graded sales with a cumulative value of $69,122. The per-sale average comes in just under $38. The bulk of submissions are commons and uncommons, not chase cards. The SIRs pull that average up but represent a small slice of what actually gets submitted. The chase tier is less crowded than the raw volume implies.</p>

<h2>Collector number is not optional</h2>

<p>Garchomp ex appears on three different cards in sv3a: AR (080/062) and SIR (085/062) are the two you'll encounter most. Pricing by artwork description alone is how you overpay or undersell. Read the collector number off the card before you list or buy. The post has this spelled out clearly for every tier.</p>
