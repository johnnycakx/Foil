<p>Sealed sv3a Raging Surf boxes are listing as low as $69.99 on TCGplayer as of August 2026. That number tells you the supply story is over. What's left is a stable SIR tier with one card still commanding serious money and a grading spread worth paying attention to.</p>

<p>The new post on the Foil blog ranks every chase card in Raging Surf by current market value, walks through the grading math on the top two SIR slots, and flags the cards that don't justify slab cost at current PSA service pricing. The short version is below. The full breakdown is at the link.</p>

<h2>The SIR tier in one pass</h2>

<p>Seven SIRs define this set. Ranked by raw price as of August 2026:</p>

<p><strong>Iono SIR (091/071): $180-$220 raw, $520-$600 PSA 10.</strong> The Supporter premium is real and structural. Iono runs four copies in competitive tournament decks across multiple formats. That demand has a floor that artwork alone can't provide, which is why the raw price hasn't compressed meaningfully since early 2025.</p>

<p><strong>Garchomp ex SIR (082/071): $95-$115 raw, $280-$320 PSA 10.</strong> The grading math here is the most interesting in the set. PSA Economy tier runs around $25 per submission in 2026. On a $100 raw card that PSA 10s at $300, you're working with roughly $175 in gross upside before platform fees. eBay takes 12-13%, TCGplayer around 10%. Defensible on a clean pull. Edge wear or surface scratches and it doesn't pencil.</p>

<p><strong>Penny SIR (092/071): $60-$80 raw, $190-$240 PSA 10.</strong></p>

<p><strong>Iron Bundle SIR (086/071): $55-$70 raw, $160-$200 PSA 10.</strong> Sits below Penny SIR despite comparable artwork quality. Lower competitive relevance post-rotation explains most of the gap. Thinner buyer pool on PSA 10 sales means you need to be confident in the grade before submitting.</p>

<p><strong>Palafin ex SIR (079/071): $40-$55 raw, $130-$160 PSA 10.</strong></p>

<p><strong>Arven SIR (090/071): $30-$45 raw, $95-$130 PSA 10.</strong></p>

<p><strong>Clodsire ex SIR (078/071): $25-$35 raw, $80-$110 PSA 10.</strong></p>

<h2>What to skip</h2>

<p>Everything outside the SIR tier is a pass for grading. Standard ex cards have settled at $3-$8 raw. PSA submission cost wipes out any realistic upside on a PSA 10 outcome. Buy them raw for the binder; don't waste a submission slot.</p>

<p>Standard Illustration Rares sit in the $8-$25 range. Worth watching as raw pickups, not as grading targets at current service pricing.</p>

<h2>One identification note worth repeating</h2>

<p>Sv3a Iono SIR (091/071) and the Iono SIR from sv4 Paradox Rift (245/182) are different cards with different prices and different artwork. Artwork recognition fails on this one. Read the set code and collector number off the card face. That's the only reliable method.</p>
