<p>The Garchomp ex SIR (086/066) in sv3a Raging Surf is sitting at $90-$120 raw NM on eBay completed sales as of mid-2026, and a clean copy grades to $280-$340 at PSA 10. That spread is real, but only if you are looking at the right card.</p>

<p>That qualifier matters because sv3a has two Garchomp ex cards: the SAR at 078/066 and the SIR at 086/066. They share a Pokémon name and similar artwork. In PSA 10, they are $200+ apart. Marketplace listings conflate them constantly. If you are buying, selling, or submitting without confirming the collector number, you are pricing blind.</p>

<p>The new post on the Foil blog ranks every chase card in the set, cross-references eBay sold listings, TCGplayer market price, and PSA auction data, and runs the grading math card by card. A few things worth noting before you go read it:</p>

<p><strong>The grading economics are tighter than they look.</strong> The Garchomp ex SIR pencils out if your raw copy is clean: $205 gross margin at current midpoints before fees, on a card where centering issues are common from this print run. A visible left-right imbalance gets you a PSA 9, and the premium collapses. The Palafin IR (074/066) at $12-$20 raw and $40-$60 PSA 10 looks like a similar multiple, but submission cost eats most of the margin at that base price. The Arven SIR (088/066) at $8-$14 raw should not be graded individually, full stop.</p>

<p><strong>Palafin ex SIR (085/066) is the underread card in the set.</strong> It has settled to $35-$50 raw after a brief spike near $70 in late 2024. PSA 10 copies are less abundant relative to demand than Garchomp ex SIR, and the grading math is proportionally more favorable. Palafin is not a legacy Pokémon, so demand here is artwork-driven rather than nostalgia-driven: slower volatility in both directions.</p>

<p><strong>Sealed product does not close the gap on singles.</strong> The sv3a booster box is ranging $70-$115 on TCGplayer as of mid-2026. A complete set of SIRs and SARs purchased individually at current raw NM prices runs roughly $175-$220. Box math on a 66-card base set with 26 extended slots does not favor set completion from sealed product. Buy the singles you want.</p>

<p><strong>Creator sentiment is color, not signal.</strong> PokeBeard has flagged the Garchomp ex SIR as a watched card, citing renewed bidding activity. The broader thesis that anniversary-set hype on Japanese product lifts secondary demand for older SV-era sets is worth monitoring if you are holding sv3a inventory. But the price floor is held by completed sales, not coverage.</p>
