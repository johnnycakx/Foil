<p>A Raging Surf booster box was listing on TCGplayer as low as $69.99 sealed as of mid-2026. If you don't hit the Garchomp ex SIR or Gardevoir ex SAR, the box loses. That's the entire risk profile of sv3a in one sentence.</p>

<p>The new post ranks every chase card in the set by current market evidence: eBay completed sales, TCGplayer market price, and PSA auction realized prices. Here's what matters before you click through.</p>

<h2>The only card with clean grade economics</h2>

<p>Garchomp ex SIR (081/071) is the headliner. Raw copies landed at <strong>$85 to $110</strong> in recent completed sales, with meaningful centering variance inside that range. PSA 10 copies cleared <strong>$220 to $260</strong> at auction. PSA 9 sits at <strong>$130 to $150</strong>, which means the PSA 10 premium here is roughly 2x the PSA 9, not the 1.3x to 1.5x you see on less-demanded SIRs. That cliff makes grading math straightforward on a visibly clean raw copy. It's the only card in the set where the economics are clearly favorable.</p>

<p>Gardevoir ex SAR (080/071) is the second heavy hitter. Raw copies sold in the <strong>$60 to $80</strong> range through mid-2026. Grade economics are borderline (the post has the full table). One practical note: the full-art border on this card makes the collector number hard to read. Confirm by set code <em>sv3a</em> and collector number 080, not artwork recognition.</p>

<h2>The card most worth sourcing in Tier 2</h2>

<p>Iono SAR (077/071) held <strong>$30 to $50</strong> raw through July 2026, with PSA 10 comps hitting <strong>$90 to $100</strong> at auction. That's the best grade-up candidate in Tier 2 if you can source raw copies with clean centering. Garchomp ex AR sits at <strong>$20 to $35</strong> raw, more liquid than the SIR but with limited upside. Grade economics on the AR are not recommended per the full analysis.</p>

<h2>What creator chatter is and isn't telling you</h2>

<p>Garchomp ex SIR was the most-discussed Raging Surf card in tracked channels over the last 30 days. PokeBeard's framing was about whether demand had a durable floor, not a buy call. When a credible channel is asking floor questions rather than calling upside, that reads as consolidation sentiment. Separately, Moonbreon and Rayquaza V Alt dominated creator airtime across the same period. Neither is a Raging Surf card. That hype bleed into sv3a search traffic is real, and it pushes impatient buyers toward overpaying on the Garchomp SIR by anchoring to alt-art comp logic from different supply contexts.</p>
